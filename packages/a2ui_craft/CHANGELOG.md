## 0.1.0-dev.1

Initial prerelease of the framework-agnostic A2UI Craft core: RFW template
parsing/AST and binary format, the reactive `DynamicContent` model, shared value
types, DTCG design-token resolution and the semantic theme-role contract, the
neutral Markdown model, and the built-in function library.

Depends on a prerelease of `a2ui_core`.
